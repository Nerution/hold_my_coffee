Privacy policy

Good Things · Last updated: [date of publication]

Good Things shows positive, sourced news. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends, and to whom.

The app is provided by [your name or company, and address] (“we”). Contact: [contact email].

Summary

  • We do not collect personal data, and we do not sell or share any.
  • Your preferences, saved stories and reading history stay on your device.
  • To show stories, the app contacts our news service and the publishers' websites. Those requests reveal your IP address, as any internet request does.

What stays on your device

The following are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • topics, regions, sources you follow or hide;
  • saved stories and collections, including copies of their pictures for offline reading;
  • reading history, the name you choose to show in the app, language, appearance, reminder and app-lock settings;
  • the latest list of stories, so the app works offline.

They are never sent to us. You can remove them at any time under Profile → Privacy → Delete my data, or by uninstalling the app.

What our news service sends to others

These are made by our server, not by your device, and contain news texts only, never your data:

  • News feeds and Google News: the server reads publishers' public feeds and Google News search results.
  • Google Translate: headlines and summaries of news stories are sent for translation when stories are shown in Polish, German or Spanish.
  • Anthropic (optional): if we turn on AI review, the texts of news stories are sent to Anthropic's Claude to judge, summarise or translate them.

Device features

  • Reminders: daily reminders are scheduled on your device by the operating system. No push service or server is involved. You can change or turn them off in the app or in your device's settings.
  • App lock: Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint and passcode checks are done by your device's operating system. The app only learns whether the check succeeded and never receives biometric data.
  • Sharing: when you share a story, the system share sheet sends what you choose to the app you pick.
  • Home-screen widget: the widget shows a headline the app has already loaded; it uses the same on-device data.

Legal basis (EU and UK)

Processing your IP address to deliver stories and pictures is necessary to provide the service you asked for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and is in our legitimate interest in running it securely (Article 6(1)(f)). We do not use data for profiling or automated decisions.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Everything the app keeps is on your device and under your control. If you have a question or want to make a request under data-protection law, write to [contact email]. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority (in Poland, the UODO).

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and describe important changes in the app's release notes.
